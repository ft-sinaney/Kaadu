Kerala sits on the wet side of the Ghats.

The mountains catch the southwest monsoon before it reaches the rest of the peninsula, and the rain that falls on this narrow strip supports one of the eight hottest biodiversity hotspots on Earth. The Western Ghats are older than the Himalaya, and a large share of what grows here grows nowhere else.

What follows is a field guide to our own campus: every tree, fruit, herb and ornamental recorded at IHRD Pazhayannur, from the teak and jackfruit to the medicinal garden, alongside the butterflies that arrive with the flowering.

The plant catalogue is our campus survey; the butterfly section covers five common species. The QR scanner decodes real codes.
